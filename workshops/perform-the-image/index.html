Perform the Image

A hand, a posture, a slightly misplaced prop: small decisions can change everything an image seems to say.

See dates & book on GetYourGuide →

The experience

Explore photography and performance through staged situations, gestures and visual metaphors. Prompts may involve expressing a mood through a fragment of the body, giving a familiar phrase a physical form, or staging a recognisable stock photograph with an awkward interruption. Making the image becomes a way to examine its assumptions.

The session welcomes non-artists and finishes with shared viewing, discussion or presentation. It is an invitation to experiment with how images construct meaning. Consult the booking option for the exercises and practical requirements of your session.
