The experience

Work with Maansi Jain on drawing prompts that loosen familiar habits of observation. Depending on the option you book, you might draw with both hands, follow a contour without looking at the page, limit a portrait to three colours, or uncover a hidden image with watercolour. Each approach gives you a different way into making an artwork.

Choose the exercise that interests you in the booking options. The session centres on that selected approach; the examples here describe the available range. You can arrive without previous drawing experience and leave with your own works on paper.
