The experience

Make the Ordinary Strange brings everyday objects into a conceptual art workshop with Maansi Jain. Depending on the session, you might shape a paper vase around a found bottle, invent a poetic museum label for something in your pocket, or write an extravagant review of an ordinary object. These experiments use sculpture, humour and language to explore how context changes meaning.
