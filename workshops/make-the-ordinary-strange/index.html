The experience

Make the Ordinary Strange brings everyday objects into a conceptual art experience with maansi jain. Depending on the session, you might shape a paper vase around a found bottle, invent a poetic museum label for something in your pocket, or write an extravagant review of an ordinary object. These experiments use sculpture, humour and language to explore how context changes meaning.

Work directly with the artist

maansi jain is a Berlin-based conceptual artist who has guest lectured at New York University (NYU Tandon and NYU Steinhardt) and brings more than two decades of teaching experience to every session. She works across photography, text, sculpture, works on paper and performance. These studio experiences extend an artistic inquiry into perception: how an instruction, an image or a material can change the way we understand what is in front of us.
