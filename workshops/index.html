Draw, translate, rearrange, perform. Four ways into contemporary art, inside an artist’s studio.

Join Maansi Jain for a hands-on encounter with drawing, language, objects or staged images. Explore the workshops, find the approach that intrigues you, and reserve your session on GetYourGuide.

Looking for something creative to do in Berlin? These 75–90 minute sessions are a hands-on way to spend an afternoon, whether you are visiting or live here. Come on your own, with a friend or as a small group. Most sessions need no art experience, and all run in English, some also in German.

Work directly with the artist

Maansi Jain is a Berlin-based conceptual artist who has guest lectured at New York University (NYU Tandon and NYU Steinhardt) and brings more than two decades of teaching experience to each workshop. She works across photography, text, sculpture, works on paper and performance. These studio workshops extend an artistic inquiry into perception: how an instruction, an image or a material can change the way we understand what is in front of us.

Before you book

How do I choose and book a workshop?

Choose a workshop below, then follow its GetYourGuide link to select the available exercise, date and number of participants. Read the selected option for precisely what your booking includes.

Do I need art experience?

Draw Otherwise, Words Become Art and Perform the Image are accessible to non-artists. Curiosity and willingness to experiment are welcome. For a particular exercise or access requirement, contact the studio before booking.

Can I come alone or with friends?

Check the participant requirements for your chosen option. Some exercises involve collaboration; minimum participant numbers may vary. You can also contact the studio with a group enquiry.

Where are the workshops and what do they cost?

Sessions take place at STUDIO JAIN in Berlin and cost €160 per person, or €50 for students. Your GetYourGuide booking shows the meeting point, available dates and cancellation terms.
