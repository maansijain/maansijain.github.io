Draw, translate, rearrange, perform. Four ways into contemporary art, inside an artist’s studio.

Join contemporary artist Maansi Jain for small-group art workshops in Berlin, with up to eight participants. All four experiences offer English-language sessions; three also offer German. Explore drawing, language, objects or staged images, then choose your option and reserve on GetYourGuide.

Work directly with the artist

Maansi Jain works across photography, text, sculpture, works on paper and performance. These studio workshops extend an artistic inquiry into perception: how an instruction, an image or a material can change the way we understand what is in front of us.

Before you book

How do I choose and book a workshop?

Choose a workshop below, then follow its GetYourGuide link to select the available exercise, date and number of participants. Read the selected option for precisely what your booking includes.

Do I need art experience?

All four workshops are accessible to non-artists. Curiosity and willingness to experiment are welcome. For a particular exercise or access requirement, contact the studio before booking.

Can I come alone or with friends?

Check the participant requirements for your chosen option. Some exercises involve collaboration; minimum participant numbers may vary. You can also contact the studio with a group enquiry.

Where are the workshops and what do they cost?

Sessions take place at STUDIO JAIN in Berlin. The selected GetYourGuide booking shows the meeting point, current price, available dates and cancellation terms. Check its access information before reserving.
