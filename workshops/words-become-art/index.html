Words Become Art

The experience

Explore how words become drawings, gestures and shared experiments. Depending on the selected option, you might translate a sentence between forms, build an image sequence around one word, or pass an unfinished instruction to someone else. The interest lies in what happens along the way: an ambiguity, an unexpected association, a meaning neither person anticipated.

This session opens questions about authorship and communication through practical experiments. It offers an accessible encounter with conceptual art for people interested in language, images and collaborative making. Check the selected booking option for its particular format.
