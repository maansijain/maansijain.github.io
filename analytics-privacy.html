Analytics privacy

Maansi Jain uses optional Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website and which links and controls they use. Contact: janusroth8@gmail.com.

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics”. Declining leaves browsing and booking links available. Use “Analytics preferences” on any page to change your choice or withdraw consent. Withdrawing stops subsequent analytics collection and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies from this website. It does not erase information already collected.

What is measured

After consent, Google Analytics receives page visits, internal and external link clicks, email and phone link clicks, button clicks and scroll milestones. Events identify the current page, link destination (without queries or fragments), link position or control identifier. Workshop clicks also identify the workshop. We do not read typed form content or record quiz answers. Email addresses and phone numbers are omitted from link events. Google also processes technical information such as browser and device details and network information, including an IP address when a request reaches its servers. This site disables Google signals and advertising personalisation. Our explicit events omit URL query strings and fragments and limit referral information to the referring website’s origin.

The _ga and _ga_* cookies distinguish browsers and sessions and are configured to expire after six months. A separate local-storage entry, mj-analytics-consent-v2, remembers your consent choice for six months. Your browser settings can remove this information earlier.

Google and external booking

Google processes analytics information on infrastructure that may be outside your country. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards for details.

A booking-link click indicates a visit to GetYourGuide. It does not confirm a reservation or payment. GetYourGuide handles the subsequent booking under its own privacy policy; this website’s analytics receives no customer names, contact details or reservation records from that process.
